# Mono luxe

One object. One sentence. Enough time to notice both. A monochrome product-film system built around shape and material, not decorative luxury signals.

## System

Warm stone and charcoal are the entire palette. Gloock provides the high-contrast serif voice; Mona Sans handles restrained supporting copy. Keep headlines to six words or fewer. Set one object on a low plinth and leave broad negative space above. The sample uses an original abstract loop sculpture, not a real product or brand endorsement.

## Motion

Reveal the object in 1.2s with `power2.out`, then uncover the headline over 0.9s. Hold for at least 3s. Prefer a hard cut on a musical phrase or one finite iris. Never add bounce, neon rims or continuous orbit merely to fill time.

## Scene recipes

1. **Silhouette:** sculpture right, quiet title left, caption on a single baseline below.
2. **Material study:** `clay-drop-3d` in monochrome, crop to the contact point, then cut to the complete form.
3. **Closing thought:** `serif-quote` with a verified attributed line, followed by `end-card` with a real destination.

Avoid faux seals, invented heritage dates, gold accents, microtype as decoration and gradients unrelated to lighting. Use real product media when supplied; never imply the abstract sample is the user's product.
