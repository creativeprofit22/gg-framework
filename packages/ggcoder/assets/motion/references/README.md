# Motion studies: behaviors, not templates

Select one relevant entry from `index.json`, then inspect its source and temporal
preview. Choose by the visible action it solves, not by palette or subject. An
ordinary brief deserves direction without asking for a showreel persona.

These small GG-authored studies connect action plans to implementation. They do
not require zoom, 3D, giant type, circles, nonstop movement or a fixed shot order.
The previews are time-ordered source renders, not full playback. A project's
approved local references may be more relevant; do not scan other folders.

`signal-study.html` is an offline, seekable reduction of GG's local signal study.
Call `seekStudy(seconds)` (0–4); transfer its coupled path/framing state into the
composition's existing GSAP timeline. It is reference source, not a replacement
HyperFrames project. The second entry points to the already-bundled library piece.

Rebuild the two JPEGs with `scripts/build-motion-references.mjs <local-gsap-file>`
from the ggcoder package. It uses installed Playwright/Sharp and blocks network
requests, substituting the supplied local GSAP for the library piece's pinned
script. No user videos, third-party logos, music or private assets are bundled.
