# Sunlit consumer

An everyday product made tangible by warm paper, a long shadow and one human sentence. Not a beige version of a tech dashboard.

## System

Cream field, chestnut ink, restrained terracotta emphasis. Fraunces carries the voice; Host Grotesk carries product details. Mono is optional and limited to dates or actual measurements. Set copy on the left and one physical-feeling product object on the right. Keep shadows directional, as though one window lights the scene.

## Motion

Settle the object over 0.9s with `power3.out`; let copy arrive 0.2s later. A slow, finite change in light can support a 3s hold. No perpetual floating. Transition by a warm field cut or a camera crop, not an unrelated particle effect.

## Scene recipes

1. **Small ritual:** the sample's paper day planner and one warm serif promise. Dates and tasks are illustrative editable copy.
2. **Product moment:** `phone-frame` on the right with a real supplied screen, one sentence on the left. Use `light-leak-soft` sparingly behind the object, never across body text.
3. **Human close:** `serif-quote` followed by `end-card`, with a concrete next step rather than invented praise.

Avoid excessive capsules, yellow-on-cream text, claims without evidence, fake testimonials and a stack of unrelated decorative objects. Do not add grain over small text.
